Measured vmax performance on the same Saturn RTL configuration (VLEN512, DLEN256). Each bar uses the median of three timed calls after one warm call; GM is the geometric mean across seven input lengths. Higher is faster; XNNPACK u8v is the fixed normalization baseline. Inputs are finite, and all measured outputs match on these inputs. The optimized and XNNPACK implementations do not propagate a single NaN like the NEON reference; their bars are performance references, not evidence of full-domain equivalence. The repaired implementation passed emulator regressions but has not received a complete formal proof. Raw samples are retained; no confidence intervals are claimed.
